What Home needs to do better

Findly stores files, photos, notes, links, and recordings, then helps people retrieve them through search and hierarchical tags. Home should offer a short route to that content. Based on the current SwiftUI source, these are the strongest opportunities:

01 / Put retrieval within reach

File-type filters and tags currently follow Recently Added, Recent, Most Used, Pinned, and optional Favorites. Move a retrieval route above the shelves so finding an older item does not depend on scrolling.

02 / Give each section a clear job

Recently Added and Recent mean different things, but use the same card layout. Label them “Recently added” and “Recently opened,” and use one switchable activity list to reduce visual repetition.

03 / Respect deliberate choices

Pinned content currently appears after three automatic shelves. Give pins priority for predictable access, and show longer titles in rows so similar documents are easier to distinguish.
